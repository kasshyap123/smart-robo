ROVER FIRMWARE — FIRST LIVE COMPONENT TEST — 17 OCTOBER 2026

The USB upload/serial test previously passed on the actual ESP32-D0WD-V3.
This OLED sketch is the next bench test; it is not complete rover firmware.

Unplug USB before wiring. Use the printed labels, not left/right pin positions:
OLED GND -> ESP32 GND
OLED VCC -> ESP32 3V3
OLED SDA -> ESP32 GPIO21 (may be labelled D21)
OLED SCL -> ESP32 GPIO22 (may be labelled D22)

Use four jumpers. A breadboard is optional for this first four-wire test.
Keep batteries, motors, motor driver, amplifier and servo power disconnected.
The ESP32 may cover all breadboard holes beside its pins; don't force a bad fit.
Direct female-to-female leads work if available. Otherwise use the breadboard
with your male-to-female leads and separate connected five-hole groups.

Sketch: OLED_Bench/OLED_Bench.ino
Board: ESP32 Dev Module, esp32:esp32:esp32; core 3.3.12 in local tooling.
Serial speed: 115200 baud. No extra display library needed for this bench test.
The sketch searches 0x3C/0x3D, writes a border, blinking eyes and a moving bar.
It retries every three seconds if the display does not respond.

An address acknowledgement does NOT prove SSD1306 identity or working pixels.
Only mark the visual test passed once the owner sees the expected full pattern.
A shifted/blank pattern after ACK may indicate a different display controller.
Do not rewire powered hardware; unplug USB first.

Next stages after this passes: microphone input, then audio output and sensors.
Motor power and Li-ion protection/charging remain unresolved; no motor-power
connection is authorized by this OLED bench wiring.
