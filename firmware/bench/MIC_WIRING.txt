INMP441 NEXT BENCH TEST — USB POWER ONLY

OLED test PASSED on 17 October 2026: ESP32 serial status says OLED data transfer
OK; owner confirms border, blinking eyes and moving bar.

Owner reports microphone has empty connection holes. Solder six short, flexible,
insulated wires to its labelled pads before connecting it. This also preserves
the flexible-wire installation selected for the CAD; a bulky header isn't needed.
Do not use loose wires pushed into the holes as electrical contacts.

Disconnect ESP32 USB before soldering or changing any connections. Work on the
unpowered microphone separately. Solder one pad at a time with electronics solder
and rosin flux. Keep joints separate and the sound port clear. Inspect for bridges
and stray wire strands. Check VDD/GND for a solder bridge before connecting power.
Use the labels printed on this board; do not infer pad order from another module.

MIC LABEL       ESP32
VDD / VCC       3V3 (not 5V)
GND             GND
SCK / BCLK      GPIO26 / D26
WS              GPIO27 / D27
SD              GPIO34 / D34
L/R             GND (select left I2S channel)

Keep the OLED wiring unchanged: SDA=21, SCL=22, VCC=3V3, GND=GND.
Use breadboard groups to share 3V3/GND. Each 5-hole group on one side is one
connection; the centre gap separates the two sides. Power rails may be split.
Use the multimeter continuity function with power disconnected to identify
connections when uncertain. Do not put different signal wires into one group.

The Mic_OLED_Bench sketch samples the selected left channel at 16 kHz in 32-bit
I2S words and computes relative RMS level from the signed 24-bit data. The OLED
keeps the eyes and displays a level bar. Serial gives relative dBFS and min/max.
This is not dB SPL and it does not recognize speech or send audio to a server.

Test once wiring is ready: speak or clap 10-20 cm away, then stay quiet. Look for
a repeatable increase and decrease in the level. Driver startup or nonzero
samples alone do not verify sound capture. Subsequent recording/playback will
be needed before signing off voice audio quality.

No new sketch is uploaded until the physical mic wiring is ready. The known-good
OLED sketch remains running. No battery, motor, amplifier or servo power added.
